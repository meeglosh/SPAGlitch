SPAGlitch 1.1.0 - what's new since 1.0.0

Two additions: a proper multiband compressor, and a calm mode for anyone
who does not want a flashing screen.

- New effect: COMP, a three-band compressor, ninth in the chain. Each band
  has a threshold, ratio, attack, release and makeup gain, and the two
  crossover points are dragged directly on the graph. Each band shows its
  own gain reduction while you play.

- Each band also has UP RATIO, which compresses upward instead of
  downward: it lifts what sits below the threshold rather than holding
  down what sits above it, which is what makes a sparse loop sound dense.
  At 1:1 it is off and the band behaves like an ordinary compressor. That
  is the default, so switching COMP on does something predictable.

- Calm mode. SPAGlitch flashes its background image on every note, which
  is a risk for anyone with photosensitive epilepsy. The CALM MODE switch
  in the header replaces the flashing with a still scene whose candles
  flicker and whose screens tear slightly as you play, and it stops the
  instrument's text glitching at the same time. It is off by default, the
  instrument offers you the choice the first time you open it, and the
  setting belongs to your machine rather than to a patch, so it stays put
  across every instance and every session.

- Eight new factory presets built around the compressor, forty-eight in
  all. The original forty are untouched.

- RANDOMIZE now rolls the compressor too, and can place it anywhere in the
  chain. Its input and output gains are deliberately left alone, as the
  master OUTPUT is, because rolling those is a level change rather than
  sound design.

- Fixed: a preset only stores the parameters that existed when it was
  saved, and loading one used to leave anything newer at whatever the
  previous patch had set. Any preset saved in 1.0.0 would have inherited
  the last patch's compressor. Parameters absent from a preset now return
  to their defaults.

- Your saved chain orders survive. The chain went from eight modules to
  nine, and a 1.0.0 patch stores only eight. Those patches keep the order
  you arranged, with the compressor inserted in front of the limiter so
  the limiter stays last.

Worth trying specifically

- COMP on a busy loop: pick a band on the graph, pull its THRESHOLD down
  and its RATIO up, and check the band's own meter moves. Then drag the
  crossovers and confirm the bands follow.

- UP RATIO on something sparse. Leave RATIO at 1:1, raise UP RATIO, and
  listen to the space between the hits fill in. This is the part we would
  most like your ear on: the thresholds are fixed internally and we have
  not tuned them against anything but our own listening.

- Calm mode, on and off, while playing. Tell us whether the candles and
  the screen tearing give you enough sense that a note landed, or whether
  it feels dead compared with the flashing version.

- Load a preset you saved in 1.0.0 and confirm its effects chain is in the
  order you left it, with COMP added and LIMIT still last.

Install notes

- macOS: signed and notarised. If the new version does not appear, use
  your host's plug-in manager to rescan, then restart it.
- Windows: unsigned. Click More info, then Run anyway, at the SmartScreen
  prompt.
