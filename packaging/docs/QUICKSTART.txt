SPAGlitch Quickstart
====================

1. FIRST SOUND
   Load SPAGlitch in your DAW (or open the standalone app) and play. All 479
   factory sounds install with it, so it makes a noise immediately - no
   library to unzip, nothing to point it at.

   The instrument plays from C3 upward. The on-screen keyboard at the bottom
   responds to your computer keyboard as well as to MIDI.

2. PICK A BANK
   SAMPLE BANK in the header holds nine banks of glitch material - Digital,
   Heavy, Squelchy, Percussive and the rest. This is the single biggest
   change you can make to the sound.

3. BROWSE THE PRESETS
   PRESETS, top left. The browser opens in its own column beside the
   instrument, so it never covers anything. Forty factory patches, live
   search, star your favourites, and SAVE to keep your own.

4. ROLL THE DICE
   The dice in the RANDOMIZE strip rolls a brand-new patch. WILD sets how
   polite or unhinged the rolls are. Lock SOUND, FILTER or FX first to hold
   that part of the patch while the rest re-rolls.

   It will never hand you a silent patch or one that is painfully loud.

5. REORDER THE CHAIN
   Drag the tabs in 03 / CHAIN. Distortion into delay is a different
   instrument from delay into distortion, and the order is saved with the
   patch. Every effect starts off - switch one on with the ON pill in its
   tab header.

6. THE EQ IS INTERACTIVE
   Double-click empty space to add a band (near the left or right edge you
   get a cut instead of a bell). Double-click a node to remove it, drag to
   move it, Cmd-drag or scroll for Q, right-click for its type and slope.

7. MIDI LEARN
   Right-click any knob to bind it to a hardware controller. The next CC you
   move is captured, and the knob wears a small CC tag afterwards.


WORTH KNOWING
-------------
  Both drawers fold. Click the 03 / CHAIN or 04 / KEYBOARD bar anywhere.

  Drag the bottom-right corner to resize. It is a pure zoom, 50 to 150%.

  The UI glitches while you play. The text tearing is tied to the x-ray
  artwork and the notes driving it, and it stops the moment the notes do.
  That is meant to happen.

  Tempo sync needs a host. In the standalone, synced delay and modulation
  rates run at a fixed 120 BPM. Switch SYNC off to hear free rates.

  CUTOFF and RESONANCE do nothing while FILTER is off. That gating is
  inherited from the original instrument.

  OUTPUT sits before the effects, so the limiter still catches the final
  signal.


SAFE LISTENING
--------------
SPAGlitch is a glitch instrument and can produce sudden, loud, transient-heavy
output, especially when you roll a new patch. Set a comfortable monitoring
level before you play.
