SPAGlitch · Silverplatter Audio
===============================

Thank you for buying SPAGlitch! You now own an unhinged glitch instrument:
nine banks of the Silverplatter Audio glitch library under a filter modelled
from the measured response of the original, a full effects chain you can
reorder by dragging its tabs, and an x-ray that fires every time you play.

SYSTEM REQUIREMENTS
-------------------
  macOS 13 or later (Apple silicon + Intel, universal binary)
  Windows 10 or later (64-bit)
  A DAW that hosts AU (macOS) or VST3 plug-ins, or use the standalone app.

  There is no AAX build, so SPAGlitch does not run in Pro Tools.


INSTALLING
----------
macOS:   open the .pkg and follow the installer. It is signed and notarized
         by Apple, so it opens with a double-click. Use Customize to choose
         formats. It installs:
           /Applications/SPAGlitch.app
           /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/SPAGlitch.component
           /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/SPAGlitch.vst3
           /Library/Application Support/Silverplatter Audio/SPAGlitch

Windows: run the setup .exe and choose the formats you want. It installs:
           C:\Program Files\Silverplatter Audio\SPAGlitch\SPAGlitch.exe
           C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\SPAGlitch.vst3
           C:\ProgramData\Silverplatter Audio\SPAGlitch\Samples

         The Windows installer is not yet code signed, so SmartScreen may
         say "Windows protected your PC" the first time you run it. Click
         More info, then Run anyway. That warning is about the absence of a
         signing certificate, not about the file.

Quit SPAGlitch and your DAW before installing. All 479 factory sounds are
included and load automatically in every format, so there is nothing else to
download and nothing to unzip.


WHAT IT DOES
------------
  Nine sample banks, 479 sounds, mapped from C3 upward.

  A filter with five modes - low pass, high pass, band pass, notch and peak -
  built from the measured response of the original instrument.

  Eight effects: distortion (soft, hard, fold, crush), chorus, delay, reverb,
  an 8-band parametric EQ, phaser/flanger, tremolo/vibrato and a limiter.
  Drag the tabs to reorder the chain; the order is part of the patch.

  Forty factory presets, and save as many of your own as you like.

  Randomize, with a wildness amount and per-group locks. It will not hand you
  a silent patch or a painfully loud one.

  MIDI learn on any knob, by right-clicking it.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
  Tempo sync needs a host. In the standalone there is no transport, so synced
  delay and modulation rates run at a fixed 120 BPM.

  The factory banks are the instrument. SPAGlitch does not load your own
  samples.

  Presets do not carry your MIDI CC bindings. Those stay with the instance,
  deliberately - they describe your hardware, not a sound.
